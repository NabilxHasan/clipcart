## How to Merge Your Voiceover Audio with the Video

Once you record your voice reading this script (e.g. `my_voiceover.wav` or `my_voiceover.mp3`), you can merge it with the pitch video in 1 command using FFmpeg:

```bash
ffmpeg -i clipcart-pitch-2min.mp4 -i my_voiceover.wav -filter_complex "[0:a][1:a]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:dropout_transition=2[aout]" -map 0:v -map "[aout]" -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k final-pitch-with-voiceover.mp4
```

*(This cleanly layers your voice over the motion sound effects while keeping the pristine 1080p 60/30fps video untouched!)*
