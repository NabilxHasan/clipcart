## 1. Keyword Strategy & Positioning Architecture

ClipCart's search strategy operates on a dual-sided marketplace model:
1. **Supply Side (Clippers/Video Editors):** High-volume, high-intent transactional queries in Bangla and Banglish focused on monetizing video editing skills, mobile editing (CapCut), and receiving local bKash payouts without Upwork/Fiverr bidding hurdles.
2. **Demand Side (Brands, Creators, Podcasters):** High-value commercial and transactional queries in English and Bangla focused on short-form distribution, organic viral reach, podcast clipping, CPM-based campaigns, and affordable alternatives to traditional Dhaka advertising agencies.
3. **Informational & Educational (The Flywheel):** High-volume informational queries explaining content clipping, CPM view verification, anti-fraud moderation, and short-form growth tactics.

### Keyword Cannibalization Rule
To prevent URL cannibalization, **each target query cluster is assigned a single authoritative owning URL**. No two pages may target the same primary keyword.

## 3. SERP Intent Analysis & Opportunity Gap in Bangladesh

### A. Supply-Side (Clippers & Editors):
- **Current SERP Landscape:** Searching for *"ভিডিও এডিটিং করে আয়"* in Bangladesh yields generic blog posts from sites like *TechTune*, *AmarDesh*, or outdated YouTube tutorials advising people to create Fiverr gigs or freelance on Upwork.
- **The Pain Point:** Young Bangladeshi editors face intense international competition on Upwork/Fiverr and high barrier to entry (dollar payments, PayPal restrictions, English proposals).
- **ClipCart's Search Advantage:** ClipCart provides an immediate, localized alternative: raw footage ready to cut, local bKash payouts starting at just ৳50, and 0% clipper commission. By capturing these queries with clear, transparent, actionable landing pages, ClipCart can dominate supply acquisition.

### B. Demand-Side (Brands, Podcasters, Founders):
- **Current SERP Landscape:** Queries like *"short form video marketing agency Bangladesh"* show high-end full-service agencies (Notionhive, Geeky Social, Libanza Films) that charge tens of thousands of Taka on monthly retainers.
- **The Pain Point:** Local podcasters, D2C startups, e-commerce stores, and personal brands cannot afford ৳50,000–৳100,000/month agency retainers, yet desperately need continuous vertical distribution on TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.
- **ClipCart's Search Advantage:** ClipCart ranks for budget-conscious commercial queries with transparent pricing: ৳1,000 micro-campaigns for 3 days, 10% platform fee, and paying strictly for human-verified CPM views.
